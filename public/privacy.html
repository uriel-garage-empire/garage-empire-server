Garage Empire: Privacy and Terms

What we store

When you create an account we store your username, your email address, and a hash of your password (or, if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID and email). We also store your game progress, your friends list, blocks, and the chat messages you send. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Why

To let you sign in, keep your progress across devices, and let players chat and add friends. Your email is never shown to other players. Other players see only your username.

Google sign-in

We only receive your verified email address and an account identifier from Google. We do not read your Gmail, contacts, or any other Google data.

Chat rules

Keep chat respectful. Messages may be filtered, reported by other players, and deleted by the game owner. Accounts that break the rules can be muted or banned.

Your data

To delete your account and data, contact the game owner at urielistired@gmail.com.

This is a hobby game and is provided as is, without any warranty.
